# Why isn’t my page in the menu?

1. Confirm that the page is present in the menu your website actually uses.
2. Check its position and indentation; it may be nested under another item.
3. Check that the page is published, within its publication period, and accessible to visitors.
4. Reload the menu editor to verify the saved structure.
5. Test the public menu on both desktop and mobile.

Creating or publishing a page does not automatically add it to a menu. A page can also belong to a collection without appearing in navigation.

If the editor tree is correct, ask your website manager whether the navigation uses a different menu, hides certain categories, or limits submenu depth.
