# Creating your first form

You need permission to create a page in the **Survey** category.

1. Choose **Make a new page or media**, enter a title, and choose **Survey**.
2. Leave **Published** unchecked and create the page.
3. In **Description**, write a short introduction: what the form is for, who should complete it, and what happens after submission.
4. Find the **Thank you text** explanation in the description's Block panel. Write a message such as “Thank you. We will contact you about your visit.”
5. Open **Questions** and add the fields you need.
6. In **Settings**, choose **Thank you text only** for the result shown after submission. Leave any special handling choice unchanged unless your website manager has supplied instructions.
7. Click **Save**, then **View**, and complete a test.

For a practice registration, ask for **Your name**, **Your email address**, and **Would you like a guided visit?** Keep questions short and ask only for information you will use.

Saving a form does not publish it. Even an unpublished preview can store a real test response, so use clearly labelled example data. Confirm the response appears under **Results** before assuming the form is ready.
