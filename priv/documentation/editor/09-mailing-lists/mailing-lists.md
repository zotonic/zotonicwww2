# Working with mailing lists

A mailing list holds or selects recipients. A mailing uses a content page as the email to send to that audience.

1. Open **Mailing lists** in the admin.
2. Find the intended list and check its title and purpose.
3. Open its recipients or list settings as needed.
4. Prepare the content page separately before starting a mailing.

Some lists use individual addresses; others also use connected people or a saved query. Check how your list selects people before sending. The total subscribers shown initially can differ from the eligible count after language, subscription, and delivery-history checks.

These tools are available only when mailing features and the relevant permissions are enabled. Follow your organization's subscription practices when adding people.
