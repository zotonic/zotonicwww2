# Creating a page

1. Click **Make a new page or media** on the dashboard or the creation button in a content overview.
2. Enter the title. Check the suggested existing pages to avoid a duplicate.
3. Choose the category that matches the content.
4. If offered, choose the content group and language. Ask your website manager if you are unsure about the group.
5. Leave **Published** unchecked while drafting.
6. Click **Create page**, add your text, and **Save**.

The available categories depend on your permissions. Choose a category for what the content is, rather than where you want it to appear. Menus and collections handle placement.
