# Titles, summaries, and body text

- **Title** names the page. Make it specific enough to recognize in search results and menus.
- **Summary** gives the main point in one or two sentences. Your site may use it in lists, previews, or introductions.
- **Content** contains the main body text.
- **Short title**, when used by your site, provides a shorter label for navigation or lists.

Write the title first, then a summary that helps the reader decide whether to open the page. Use short paragraphs and descriptive subheadings in the body.

Save and view the page. A field can be stored correctly without appearing in your site's design. If a summary is missing from the public page, check with your website manager before copying it into several fields.
