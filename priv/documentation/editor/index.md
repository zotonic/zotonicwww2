# Zotonic for editors

Use this guide to write, organize, and publish content in Zotonic's admin.
Start with **Getting started**, or choose a collection below. Use **Surveys and
forms** to collect feedback, take registrations, and follow up on responses.

The screenshots show the standard admin on a test website. Your site may use
different labels, categories, or layouts. Some tools are available only when
your website enables them and your account has permission.

Saving edits to a published page can update the website immediately. Use an
unpublished page while learning or preparing new content.
