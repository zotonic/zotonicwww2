# Understanding relationships

Open a page's **Connected to** tab to see the content it points to. Open **Connected from** to see the content that points to it.

For an article connected to a person as its author:

- On the article, the person is an outgoing **Author** connection.
- On the person, the article appears as an incoming connection.

The connection name tells you what the relationship means. Direction matters: “this article has this author” is different from “this person has this article as an author”.

Use **Connections** or **Referrers** near the page title for a wider overview. Before deleting shared content, inspect its incoming connections. A text link or external use may not be represented in these lists.
